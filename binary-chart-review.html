What Home Assistant does differently

  • Categorical y axis: the history graph labels each state (“Open”, “Closed”) on the axis, so the mark explains itself.
  • Stepped, state-coloured series with a soft fill, not one flat block; a state change is a vertical edge you can see.
  • Tooltip with duration (“Closed for 2 hours 12 minutes”) on hover, and a state summary above the chart.
  • Aligned time ticks on the hour/day, with the range in the header rather than clamped at the edges.
  • A logbook/list of exact transitions next to the graph for brief or sub-pixel events.
