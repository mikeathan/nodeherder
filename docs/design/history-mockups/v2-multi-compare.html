Sample 2 — multi-series, legend, thresholds, compare

The transport already sends an array of exposes (HistoryRequest.exposes, backend exposes[]), but the UI only ever asks for one. This sample shows what overlapping series would look like, plus comparison and annotations.

  • Two series, one window: measured temperature and its target setpoint (same unit, no second axis needed).
  • Legend toggles hide or show a series without losing the query.
  • Compare overlays the same window from the previous day as a dashed ghost.
  • Threshold band (comfort 18–24 °C) and an automation marker explain why the line moved.
  • Mixed units (temperature + humidity) would need a second y-axis — shown here as a deliberate decision, not a free win.
