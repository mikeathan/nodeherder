Sample 5 — from a tile, to a sheet, to full history

Today the entity dialog always shows “Last 4 hours” with no way forward, and history lives in a device tab. This is the phone path: tap a tile, read the sheet, open full history in one tap.

  • Sheet keeps the confirmed value, one control and a 170 px chart; actions sit above the safe area.
  • View full history is a primary action, not a hidden tab.
  • Full screen repeats the same chart model at 220 px with range chips and a 2×2 stats grid.
  • Drag-to-dismiss and the bottom safe area are the missing platform pieces today (viewport-fit=cover is absent, so env(safe-area-inset-*) is 0).
