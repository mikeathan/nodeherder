Sample 3 — chart control and expansion

Spec 007 T211 asks for “inspection/zoom/reset” and never shipped: only chart.js is installed, with no zoom plugin, and the x-axis is hard-limited to three ticks. This sample is the control study.

  • Zoom controls − + Reset for keyboard and touch users, drag a region with the pointer, wheel to zoom on desktop, double-click to reset.
  • Expand opens a full-screen chart layer with its own bar and a Close button — the current chart is 235 px and cannot grow.
  • Chart type: line, step (state-like) and bars over one model.
  • Automation marker explains a jump in the line.
  • Target behaviour on phones: two-finger pinch. Drag-to-zoom stands in for it here.

Decision to make: is a zoom plugin worth ~8 kB, or are preset ranges plus drag-zoom enough?
