Sample 1 — discoverable history, bigger chart

Today the entity and period are two selects inside a History tab. This sample makes both visible and one tap away, uses the full content width, and keeps a value readout above the chart.

  • Range chips replace the period select, including Today and Yesterday which the plan (spec 007 T211) requires and the app lacks.
  • Entity chips show every chartable expose with its sensor colour, so nobody has to guess what is selectable.
  • Full-width, 360 px tall chart instead of 235 px inside a 64 rem column.
  • Readout above the chart (hover or drag) so touch users are not dependent on a transient tooltip.
  • Stats strip (min / average / max / last) instead of a two-row definition list.

Decision to make: chips vs select on desktop, and whether history gets its own nav entry or stays inside a device.
