One primitive: Section

No “Services type” and no “single-card type”. A section has a name, a width, a view and one or more cards. That is the whole model.

Home ├── Section “Living room” 1 col · cards │ ├── Card device 3u │ └── Card device 4u ├── Section “Services” 2 cols · list │ ├── Row bins │ ├── Row weather │ └── Row air quality └── Section “Weather” 1 col · cards └── Card weather 4u ← one card, still has a header

A single-card section is normal. It gets a name, so it can never be the unlabelled orphan from the screenshot.

View is per section

  • Cards — the spatial view. Explicit width (1–2 columns) and bounded height (2–5 units). Best for a few things you scan.
  • List — one compact row per card, ~42px. Best for many services or many device values. Ignore width/height, keep them stored for when you switch back.
  • One or many — a section with one card and a section with nine use the same header and the same switch.
  • No third level — if you want grouping, make another section. “Split by type” just creates sections for you.

Same six services, two views

Cards

Spatial, scannable, variable geometry. Six services occupy two rows in a wide section. Good when each service shows a different kind of value.

List

Dense and uniform. Six rows in two columns, one line each. Good when you mainly need “what is the value right now”.

Switching

Order, card width and height are preserved. List ignores geometry, cards restore it. No data or placement changes.

Single-card section

Same header, same views. A one-card list is allowed but cards are the sensible default.

Can a group have one card?

Yes. It has a header, count “1”, add, collapse and view switch. The card does not stretch to fill the group.

Do groups behave like device groups?

Exactly: name, order, collapse, width, and now a view. Delete asks what to do with its cards.

Does a big Services section dominate?

Switch it to list (3 rows per column at 6 services) or split it. Cards are bounded; the list is dense.

What if I switch back?

Widths and heights are preserved per card, so the card view returns exactly as it was.
