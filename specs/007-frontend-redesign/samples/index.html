NodeHerder redesign — design samples

Round 3. Hearth + Panel is the recommended merge: Hearth for every page, with the NodeHerder logo and a phone-friendly automation editor, plus Deck’s big-dial look as a full-screen panel mode for a wall tablet. Hearth and Deck are kept for comparison. All three share one set of ten themes (palette icon → Palette), each in light and dark. Open All pages to see every screen on one scroll.

What to look at

  • Panel mode: “Panel mode” in the sidebar or on Home. One room per page (arrows or the pager), temperature dial, big light buttons, Status gauges; “Exit panel” goes back. Tapping a sensor opens the normal device page.
  • Recent activity on Overview has a Show switch. Off hides the feed and stops collecting it; the choice is remembered in this browser.
  • Automation editor on a phone: When / If / Then stack vertically, every field is full width, and the save bar stays at the bottom.
  • Themes: Daylight, Teak, Avocado (from Hearth), Lagoon, Ember, Frost (from Deck), and new Fleece (from the logo), Fjord, Plum, Graphite.
  • Logo: top of the sidebar, in the phone header, on the sign-in page and at the left of the panel clock strip.
